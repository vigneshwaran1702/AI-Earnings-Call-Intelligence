# TCS — Q3 FY26 AI Earnings Summary

## 1. Executive Summary
TCS delivered a bullish Q3 FY26 performance with ₹65,210 Cr (+5.6% YoY) and ₹12,480 Cr (+8.2% YoY).

## 2. Financial Performance
Revenue: ₹65,210 Cr (+5.6% YoY) | PAT: ₹12,480 Cr (+8.2% YoY) | Margin: 24.8% Operating Margin.

## 3. Segmental & Operational Drivers
Core verticals showed steady momentum with strong order/customer traction during Q3 FY26.

## 4. Management Guidance & Outlook
Management expects BFSI recovery in North America and sustained GenAI deal pipeline momentum into FY27.

## 5. Capital Allocation & Cash Flow
Healthy cash generation and disciplined balance-sheet management continued through the quarter.

## 6. Key Risks & Headwinds
Discretionary IT spending caution in European retail; Wage inflation and visa-related cost pressures in North America

## 7. Analyst Q&A Takeaways
Analysts focused on margin durability, demand recovery, and medium-term capital efficiency.
