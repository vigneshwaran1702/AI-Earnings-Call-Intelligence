# Infosys — Q3 FY26 AI Earnings Summary

## 1. Executive Summary
Infosys delivered a bullish Q3 FY26 performance with ₹41,764 Cr (+6.1% YoY) and ₹6,890 Cr (+7.5% YoY).

## 2. Financial Performance
Revenue: ₹41,764 Cr (+6.1% YoY) | PAT: ₹6,890 Cr (+7.5% YoY) | Margin: 21.3% Operating Margin.

## 3. Segmental & Operational Drivers
Core verticals showed steady momentum with strong order/customer traction during Q3 FY26.

## 4. Management Guidance & Outlook
FY26 constant-currency revenue growth guidance raised to 4.5%–5.0% on strong Topaz AI adoption.

## 5. Capital Allocation & Cash Flow
Healthy cash generation and disciplined balance-sheet management continued through the quarter.

## 6. Key Risks & Headwinds
Macro uncertainty in telecom and hi-tech verticals; Cross-currency headwinds impacting reported USD growth

## 7. Analyst Q&A Takeaways
Analysts focused on margin durability, demand recovery, and medium-term capital efficiency.
