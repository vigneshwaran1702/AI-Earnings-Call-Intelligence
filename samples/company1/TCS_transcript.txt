2. OPENING REMARKS & FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE
Good evening everyone, and welcome to the TCS Q3 FY26 earnings conference call.
We are pleased to report another resilient quarter driven by disciplined execution and client trust.

Key Financial Metrics:
  - Revenue: ₹65,210 Cr (+5.6% YoY) — "Q3 revenue grew 5.6% YoY to ₹65,210 crore, led by BFSI and Consumer Business."
  - Operating Margin: 24.8% (+40 bps QoQ) — "Disciplined pyramid optimization helped expand operating margin to 24.8%."
  - Net Profit (PAT): ₹12,480 Cr (+8.2% YoY) — "Net income stood at ₹12,480 crore with strong cash conversion."
  - Total Contract Value (TCV): $10.2B (+12% YoY) — "Order book TCV reached $10.2 billion in Q3, including two mega-deals."

3. SEGMENTAL & OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
Across our core operating segments, demand visibility improved sequentially. Digital transformation,
operational efficiency, and disciplined capital allocation remained central to our strategy during
Q3 FY26. Management highlighted strong customer retention and healthy pipeline conversion.

4. MANAGEMENT GUIDANCE & OUTLOOK
Management expects BFSI recovery in North America and sustained GenAI deal pipeline momentum into FY27.

5. KEY RISKS & WATCHLIST
  - Discretionary IT spending caution in European retail
  - Wage inflation and visa-related cost pressures in North America

6. Q&A HIGHLIGHTS

We remain focused on balancing growth investments with healthy free cash flow generation.
